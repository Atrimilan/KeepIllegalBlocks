#### Overview

* Added support for Minecraft `26.1.x` (`1.20.6` to `1.21.11` are still supported).
* Consolidated "fragile" and "connectable" into the new "reactive" type. "Connectable" is now a property of reactive
  blocks. Documentation is updated accordingly.
* Reworked the restoration service to fix issues where connectable reactive blocks were not always properly restored.
  _Some edge cases may still (rarely) occur in very specific situations, as the restoration system implemented is still
  fairly lightweight. Advanced chunk management would have been overkill for what the plugin does._
* Added `leaf-litters` as a reactive block.
* Added propagation of material classifications to placement materials to prevent duplication when item and block
  materials differ. For example, `Material.REDSTONE` becomes `Material.REDSTONE_WIRE` once placed.
* Added `Material.AMETHYST_SHARD` to the `amethyst-clusters` category (to prevent item duplication).
* Added `Material.CHORUS_FRUIT` to the `chorus-plants` category (to prevent item duplication).
